Harbor Freight Roadshock 14 in. Spot/Flood Combo LED Light Bar (SKU 64321) - Combo Beam Pattern and Specs

Harbor Freight · SKU 64321 · Printed on the retail box · Photographed October 2026

Combo beam pattern chart and specs from the Roadshock 14 in. light bar box (SKU 64321): 360 lx at 30 ft, 1 lx at 600 ft, 0.25 lx at 1000 ft.

Lux (lx) is how much light lands on a surface. For reference: full moon about 0.1–0.3 lx, a candle 3 ft away about 1 lx, a lit street 10–20 lx, a living room 50–150 lx, an office 300–500 lx, an overcast day 1,000+ lx. Advertised “beam distance” is usually measured to 0.25 lx (the ANSI FL1 standard), about full-moon brightness, so treat it as the edge of usable light, not where the beam is still bright.

This is the beam pattern chart printed on the retail box of the Harbor Freight Roadshock 14 in. Spot/Flood Combo LED Light Bar, item 64321. Harbor Freight's product page lists the lumens and the 1115 ft. range, but it doesn't show this chart or any lux readings.

The chart is a top-down view of the beam. The light levels printed along the top line up with the distances printed along the bottom: 360 lx at 30 ft, 1 lx at 600 ft, and 0.25 lx at 1000 ft. The side scale runs 90 ft to each side of center. The combo pattern is wide close to the light and narrows into a long spot beam.

The box also lists lumens, beam distance, color temperature, voltage, and wattage (below). Those match the product page.
