About this site

Sometimes the one detail you need, like a beam pattern, a wiring diagram, a spec table, or a size chart, is printed right on the box, shelf tag, or label in the store, but it's nowhere to be found online. It isn't on the manufacturer's site, the retailer's product page, or the downloadable manual.

When I find information like that in person, I photograph it and post it here, one page per item, so the next person searching for it can actually find it.

How entries are made

  • Every photo is my own, taken in person.
  • Photos are cleaned before posting: location and camera data is removed, and anything personal (faces, receipts, people's names) is cropped or blurred out.
  • Printed text is transcribed as accurately as I can. Anything I couldn't read clearly is marked as such instead of guessed.
  • Each entry links to the official sources I checked, so you can see what is and isn't published there.
