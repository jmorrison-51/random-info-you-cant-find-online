Harbor Freight Roadshock 8 in. Spot/Flood Combo LED Light Bar (SKU 64324) - Combo Beam Pattern and Specs

Harbor Freight · SKU 64324 · Printed on the retail box · Photographed October 2026

Combo beam pattern chart and specs from the Roadshock 8 in. light bar box (SKU 64324): 110 lx at 30 ft, 1 lx at 350 ft, 0.25 lx at 600 ft.

This is the beam pattern chart printed on the retail box of the Harbor Freight Roadshock 8 in. Spot/Flood Combo LED Light Bar, item 64324. Harbor Freight's product page lists the lumens and the 696 ft. range, but it doesn't show this chart or any lux readings.

The chart is a top-down view of the beam. The light levels printed along the top line up with the distances printed along the bottom: 110 lx at 30 ft, 1 lx at 350 ft, and 0.25 lx at 600 ft. The side scale runs 90 ft to each side of center.

The box also lists lumens, beam distance, color temperature, voltage, and wattage (below). Those match the product page.
