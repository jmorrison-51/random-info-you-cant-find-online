Harbor Freight Roadshock Edge 4-LED Off-Road Spotlight (SKU 59235) - Spot Beam Pattern and Specs

Harbor Freight · SKU 59235 · Printed on the retail box · Photographed October 2026

Spot beam pattern chart and spec table from the Roadshock Edge 4-LED Off-Road Spotlight box (SKU 59235): 3200 lx at 30 ft, 1 lx at 1860 ft, 0.25 lx at 3700 ft.

Lux (lx) is how much light lands on a surface. For reference: full moon about 0.1–0.3 lx, a candle 3 ft away about 1 lx, a lit street 10–20 lx, a living room 50–150 lx, an office 300–500 lx, an overcast day 1,000+ lx. Advertised “beam distance” is usually measured to 0.25 lx (the ANSI FL1 standard), about full-moon brightness, so treat it as the edge of usable light, not where the beam is still bright.

This is the spot beam pattern chart and specification table printed on the retail box of the Harbor Freight Roadshock Edge 4-LED Off-Road Spotlight, item 59235. Harbor Freight's product page gives a single figure, 3721 lux at 10 meters, but it doesn't show the beam pattern chart.

The chart is a top-down view of the beam. The light levels printed along the top line up with the distances printed along the bottom: 3200 lx at 30 ft, 1 lx at 1860 ft, and 0.25 lx at 3700 ft. The side scale runs 150 ft to each side of center. The pattern is very wide right in front of the light and then narrows into a long, thin spot beam.

The box lists three light modes: daytime amber running light, low (spot), and high speed spot. It also gives a high beam max. distance of 3700 ft., a low beam max. distance of 1800 ft., and an amp draw at 12 V of 8 A high and 5.5 A low. The box notes that a light harness is required and sold separately.
