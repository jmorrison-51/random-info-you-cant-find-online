Harbor Freight Roadshock 3 in. LED Spotlight (SKU 64323) - Spot Beam Pattern and Specs

Harbor Freight · SKU 64323 · Printed on the retail box · Photographed October 2026

Spot beam pattern chart, specs, and dimensions from the Roadshock 3 in. LED Spotlight box (SKU 64323): 220 lx at 30 ft, 1 lx at 480 ft, 0.25 lx at 970 ft.

This is the spot beam pattern chart printed on the retail box of the Harbor Freight Roadshock 3 in. LED Spotlight, item 64323. Harbor Freight's product page lists 1050 lumens and a 960 ft. range, but it doesn't show this chart or any lux readings, and neither does the downloadable manual.

The chart is a top-down view of the beam. The light levels printed along the top line up with the distances printed along the bottom: 220 lx at 30 ft, 1 lx at 480 ft, and 0.25 lx at 970 ft. The side scale runs 90 ft to each side of center. The beam is long and narrow, staying within about 30 ft of center.

The dimension drawing shows a 3 in. wide, 3 in. deep housing that is 3 in. tall, or 3.5 in. including the mounting bracket. The box lists the wattage as 15, while the product page lists 15.1.
