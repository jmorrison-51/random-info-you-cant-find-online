Harbor Freight Roadshock 3 in. LED Flood with Side Light, Pair (SKU 57539) - Flood Beam Pattern and Dimensions

Harbor Freight · SKU 57539 · Printed on the retail box · Photographed October 2026

Per-light flood beam pattern and dimensions from the Roadshock 3 in. LED Flood with Side Light box (SKU 57539): 81 lx at 32 ft to 0.25 lx at 590 ft.

Lux (lx) is how much light lands on a surface. For reference: full moon about 0.1–0.3 lx, a candle 3 ft away about 1 lx, a lit street 10–20 lx, a living room 50–150 lx, an office 300–500 lx, an overcast day 1,000+ lx. Advertised “beam distance” is usually measured to 0.25 lx (the ANSI FL1 standard), about full-moon brightness, so treat it as the edge of usable light, not where the beam is still bright.

This is the beam pattern chart and dimension drawing printed on the retail box of the Harbor Freight Roadshock 3 in. LED Flood with Side Light, Pair, item 57539. Harbor Freight's product page lists 2120 lumens and a 590 ft. range, but it doesn't show this chart or any lux readings.

The chart shows the flood beam of one light and notes that the side light beam isn't included. The chart is a top-down view of the beam. The light levels printed along the top line up with the distances printed along the bottom: 81 lx at 32 ft, 1 lx at 295 ft, and 0.25 lx at 590 ft. The side scale is marked 32, 65, and 98 ft to each side of center.

The drawing gives the light's size as 3.81 wide, 3.5 tall, and 2.89 deep, with no units printed. The matching spot version's box (SKU 57538) shows the same numbers in inches. The product page lists the dimensions as 3.5 in. high, 2.89 in. long, and 4.81 in. wide.
