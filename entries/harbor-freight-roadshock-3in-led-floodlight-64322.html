Harbor Freight Roadshock 3 in. LED Floodlight (SKU 64322) - Flood Beam Pattern and Specs

Harbor Freight · SKU 64322 · Printed on the retail box · Photographed October 2026

Flood beam pattern chart, specs, and dimensions from the Roadshock 3 in. LED Floodlight box (SKU 64322): 25 lx at 30 ft, 1 lx at 160 ft, 0.25 lx at 300 ft.

Lux (lx) is how much light lands on a surface. For reference: full moon about 0.1–0.3 lx, a candle 3 ft away about 1 lx, a lit street 10–20 lx, a living room 50–150 lx, an office 300–500 lx, an overcast day 1,000+ lx. Advertised “beam distance” is usually measured to 0.25 lx (the ANSI FL1 standard), about full-moon brightness, so treat it as the edge of usable light, not where the beam is still bright.

This is the flood beam pattern chart printed on the retail box of the Harbor Freight Roadshock 3 in. LED Floodlight, item 64322. Harbor Freight's product page lists 965 lumens and a 335 ft. range, but it doesn't show this chart or any lux readings.

The chart is a top-down view of the beam. The light levels printed along the top line up with the distances printed along the bottom: 25 lx at 30 ft, 1 lx at 160 ft, and 0.25 lx at 300 ft. The side scale runs 90 ft to each side of center. The pattern is a wide oval, typical of a flood beam.

The dimension drawing shows a 3 in. wide, 3 in. deep housing that is 3 in. tall, or 3.5 in. including the mounting bracket. The box also lists lumens, beam distance, color temperature, voltage, and wattage (below).
