Harbor Freight Roadshock 3 in. LED Spot with Side Light, Pair (SKU 57538) - Spot Beam Pattern and Dimensions

Harbor Freight · SKU 57538 · Printed on the retail box · Photographed October 2026

Per-light spot beam pattern and dimensions from the Roadshock 3 in. LED Spot with Side Light box (SKU 57538): 473 lx at 32 ft to 0.25 lx at 1427 ft.

This is the beam pattern chart and dimension drawing printed on the retail box of the Harbor Freight Roadshock 3 in. LED Spot with Side Light, Pair, item 57538. Harbor Freight's product page lists 2090 lumens and a 960 ft. range, but it doesn't show this chart or any lux readings.

The chart shows the spot beam of one light and notes that the side light beam isn't included. The chart is a top-down view of the beam. The light levels printed along the top line up with the distances printed along the bottom: 473 lx at 32 ft, 1 lx at 712 ft, and 0.25 lx at 1427 ft. The side scale is marked 32, 65, and 98 ft to each side of center. The beam is long and narrow.

The drawing gives the light's size as 3.81 in. wide, 3.5 in. tall, and 2.89 in. deep. The product page lists the dimensions as 3.79 in. high, 3.21 in. long, and 2.94 in. wide.
